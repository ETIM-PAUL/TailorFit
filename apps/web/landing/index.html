How it works

  1. Your tailor sends you a link. Open it on your phone.
  2. Take two photos. Stand straight in fitted clothes: one from the front, one from the side. Add a photo of your fabric to see the outfit.
  3. Your tailor gets your measurements. Each number carries a confidence mark, and your tailor checks them before cutting.

Your photos

  • Your face is blurred automatically.
  • Photos are deleted right after processing, unless you choose to let your tailor keep them.
  • You can ask for your photos to be deleted at any time.

Measurements are estimates made from photos. Your tailor confirms them before cutting.
