How it works

  1. Add your customer

    Their name, the style and the fabric width. You get a private link to send them on WhatsApp.

  2. They take two photos

    Front and side, standing straight in fitted clothes. The camera tells them when their whole body is in view.

  3. You check and cut

    Tape the numbers marked to check, adjust the ease, and print the cutting sheet with the yardage.

Questions

Does my customer need to install anything?

No. They open the link you send in their phone's browser, take two photos and enter their height.

Can I cut straight from these numbers?

Not without checking. Use the confidence marks: tape the numbers marked low or listed under "Tape these first", and treat the rest as a close starting point.

What do my customers need to do for good photos?

Stand straight in fitted clothes, arms slightly away from the body, with the phone upright at about waist height. The camera screen guides them and says when their whole body is in view.

Which styles does it support?

Thirteen to start: agbada, senator, kaftan, buba and sokoto, gown, mermaid gown, iro and buba, bubu, skirt and blouse, jumpsuit, dashiki, shirt and trousers. Each has its own ease and fabric calculation, and you can adjust both per order.

What happens to the photos?

They're used to work out the measurements and then deleted, unless the customer ticks the box to let you keep face-blurred copies.
