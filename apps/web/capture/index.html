TailorFit

Before we start

TailorFit takes two photos of you and your height, and gives your tailor a measurement sheet.

  • Your photos are used only to work out your measurements.
  • Your face is blurred automatically before anything is stored or shown.
  • While you line up, small preview frames are checked on our server to tell you if your whole body is in view. They are not stored.
  • Photos are deleted after processing unless you choose to keep them. You can ask for deletion at any time.
  • Measurements are estimates. Your tailor checks them before cutting.

Your height

We use your height to get the scale right. Measure without shoes if you can.

    On a laptop? Put it at waist height (on a chair or a box), tilt the screen until the camera looks straight at you, not up, and stand 2 to 3 metres back. It's much easier on a phone: open this same link there.

    The live camera isn't available here, so take the photo with your phone's camera app instead.
